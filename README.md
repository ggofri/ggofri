# ggofri

## Founder of bosun.sh · AI Engineering, Research & Developer Tools

I build tools and run experiments to understand how AI changes software development: where agents help, where they fail, and what evidence we need before trusting a system.

The questions turn into **code, evaluations and research**. Sometimes the result is a tool worth keeping. Sometimes it's a reason to stop building.

**⚙️ Focused on:** Product Engineering · AI Engineering · Applied AI · Forward Deployed Engineering · Growth Engineering

**Also:** Developer Experience & Platform Engineering

### 🔬 Research & Experiments

- [**The Intervention Penalty**](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.19457265) — a simulation-based preprint exploring the costs of human checkpoints in AI coding workflows. I wrote the initial draft; Sofia Plajutin edited.
- [**AI in Cybersecurity**](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.19463970) — a coauthored evidence study separating bounded capability results from confidence in autonomous security operations. My contribution covered experiments, research and examples.
- **Engineering experiments:** Model integrations, orchestration, evaluations and comparisons with simpler approaches. I'm interested in what the evidence supports—and what it leaves unanswered.

Explore the supporting code, data and research materials in [**Bosun Papers**](https://github.com/bosun-sh/papers).

### 🛠️ Things I'm Building

At **Bosun**, I work on tools for AI-assisted development:

- [**Logbook**](https://github.com/bosun-sh/logbook) — a filesystem kanban for AI agents: shared task state, durable context, lifecycle hooks and Linear sync.
- [**Ohtools**](https://github.com/bosun-sh/ohtools) — a TypeScript framework for tools agents can inspect before executing, with typed contracts and a shared registry exposed through CLI and MCP.

Also at Bosun: [Chest](https://github.com/bosun-sh/chest), [Plot](https://github.com/bosun-sh/plot), [Sounder](https://github.com/bosun-sh/sounder) and [Skills](https://github.com/bosun-sh/skills).

Outside Bosun, I built [**Fake-End**](https://github.com/ggofri/fake-end): local HTTP mocks from YAML and TypeScript, with file-based routes, request interpolation and guarded responses.

### Production Work
